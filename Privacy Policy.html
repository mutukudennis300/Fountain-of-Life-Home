Privacy Policy

How this website handles information you submit through its forms

1. Introduction

This notice describes information you may provide when using this website. It does not describe records the home may keep outside the website.

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

2. Photos on This Website

Photos and stories are published on this website. If you have a concern about an image or information shown here, please contact us using the details below and identify the relevant page.

3. Information You Submit

The contact form asks for your name, email address, phone number (optional), subject and message. The success story feedback form asks for your name, email address and feedback. The volunteer form asks for your name, email address, phone number, interests and, optionally, experience and skills.

Contact and feedback submissions are handled by FormSubmit, while volunteer applications are handled by Formspree. Those providers process the information you submit under their own privacy policies. Please do not include sensitive personal information or private details about a child in a website form.

This website does not provide an online card-payment form. Donation information directs visitors to contact the home or use the external payment options described on the donation page.

4. How Submitted Information Is Used

Information submitted through the contact, feedback and volunteer forms is provided so the home can receive and respond to your inquiry, message or volunteer application. The form providers' handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

This website links to or loads services provided by other organizations, including form providers, Google Maps, Google Fonts, WhatsApp and social media platforms. When you use those services, they may receive information from your browser or from information you choose to submit. Their own privacy policies apply.

6. Cookies and Technical Information

This website does not describe or guarantee the cookie or data practices of external services it links to or loads. Those providers may collect technical information or use cookies under their own policies. Please review the relevant provider's privacy information for details.

7. Information About Children

The website forms are for contact and volunteer inquiries. Do not use them to submit private or sensitive information about a child. For a concern about information or an image published on this website, contact the home directly.

8. Data Security

Contact and volunteer form submissions are handled by the external providers identified above. This website does not make claims about those providers' security controls or about the home's offline record-keeping practices. Avoid sending sensitive information through the website.

9. Questions About Information You Submitted

For questions about information you submitted through this website, contact the home using the details below. For information held by FormSubmit, Formspree or another external service, you may also need to contact that provider.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this notice as the website changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact Us
