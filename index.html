Our story

A simple act of care grew into a place to call home.

Our journey began in 2000 with a feeding initiative in Pumwani. That simple act of care grew into a wider commitment to supporting vulnerable boys with stability, education and hope.

Today, the home cares for 40 boys. We believe every child deserves dignity, opportunities to learn and people who care about what comes next.
